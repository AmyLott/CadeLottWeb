About Cade

Great writing—whether for a federal grant panel, a high school classroom, or a literary journal—starts with understanding human stakes.

My path to the page hasn’t been linear. Before earning my MFA and stepping into special education and curriculum development, I worked in law enforcement and criminal investigation, trained horses, and navigated the hard-earned lessons of real life—both the triumphs and the fractures. Those experiences inform everything I write.

An international literary competition winner at fourteen and a published writer ever since, I now partner with nonprofits, educators, and organizations as a grant writing and curriculum consultant, while continuing to publish fiction, arts journalism, and personal non-fiction.
